Lesson Date: January 20, 2026

What is Cell ?

A cell is the smallest unit of life that can function independently.

All living organisms are made of cells. Every cell contains genetic material.

"The cell is the basic unit of life."
- Cell Biology Textbook

Key Point: Cells are discovered with a microscope.

Types of Cells

There are two main types:

  • Prokaryotic cells (bacteria, no nucleus)
  • Eukaryotic cells (animals, plants, fungi)

Cells Structures

Important structures include:

  • Nucleus: Contains DNA
  • Mitochondria: Powerhouse of the cell
  • Ribosome: Make proteins
  • Cell Membrane: Controls what enters/exits

Remember: Each structure has a specific function.

Common Misconception

ALL cells are visible to the naked eye
Most cells need a microscope to see

Red blood cells have a nucleus
Red blood cells do NOT have a nucleus

Quiz Question

Student asked: How big is a typical cell?

Answer: About 100 micrometers in diameter.
