5 Best Study Methods

posted on July 11, 2026

Method 1: Active Recall

Active recall means testing yourself on material you've learning. This method is proven to work better then passive reading.

"Testing us one of the most effective study techniques."
-Learning Science Research

Important: Quiz yourself without looking at notes.

Method 2: Spaced Repetition

Spaced repetition is reviewing material at increasing intervals.

Don't study everything the night before
Study a little bit every day

Method 3: The Feynman Technique

Explain concepts as if teaching someone else. Simple explanations show true understanding.

Named after physicist R. Feynman
